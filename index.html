Tool 01

Hammer Recommending Index

Which hammer puts the least strain on the body for the job in front of you?

  • Set the material, hammer weight and task length
  • Ranks the study hammers by overall strain
  • Breaks the score into muscle effort, impact force, muscular fatigue and overall workload
Open tool Tool 02

Hammering Exposure Tracker

How much exposure is a shift with this hammer actually producing?

  • Clock a work day: hammering, rest, back to hammering
  • Draws the shift as a square wave of active and off time
  • Totals the active time, rest time and duty cycle
