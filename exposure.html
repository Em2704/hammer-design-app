Notes on the shift setup

  1. * Hammer weight — head weight of the hammer used for this shift. It is written into the day's record and locked once the clock starts. This tool times the shift; it does not score the weight — use the Hammer Recommending Index for that.

Hammering Total time the hammer was in use: the sum of every hammering segment, from each press of Start / Resume to the next Pause or Finish. Timing only — no exposure score is applied. 00:00
Rest Total time the hammer was down: the sum of every rest segment, from each Pause to the next Resume or Finish. 00:00
Bouts Number of separate hammering segments in the shift. Every Start or Resume opens a new bout; the longest one is reported in the shift summary. 0
Duty cycle Share of the shift spent hammering: hammering time ÷ (hammering + rest), as a percentage. 100% means no rest was recorded. It describes the work pattern only; no risk threshold is applied. —
